16 note alert
How to play^L^L
Pieces move one square diagonally forward onto an
empty dark square.  A piece jumps an enemy piece
next to it when the square beyond is empty, and
takes it.  Jumps must be taken, and a piece keeps
jumping while it can.  A piece that reaches the far
row becomes a king (a ring in the middle) and may
then move and jump backward too.^L^L
Click your piece, then the square it goes to; for a
jump, where it lands.  Dots show where it can go.^L^L
You win when the Lisa cannot move: all its pieces
taken or blocked.
